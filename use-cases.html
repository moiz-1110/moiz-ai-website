Orders and mail we automate

Reads orders from email and PDF

Challenge. Orders arrive by email and PDF and are typed in by hand.

What we build. An AI flow reads each order, matches customer and products, and creates it in your system.

Result. Less typing, fewer errors, and staff free for other work.

Answers the inbox and complaints

Challenge. Mailboxes full of questions and complaints that need sorting and answering.

What we build. AI classifies each message, drafts a reply from your own data and passes difficult cases to a person.

Result. Quick, consistent replies, and nothing slips through.

Enters orders at the customer

Challenge. An account manager has to go back to the office to place an order.

What we build. An internal app where the account manager creates the order for the customer on the spot.

Result. The order is placed during the visit, with the right prices.

Data, customers and files

Keeps product and customer data equal

Challenge. Product and customer information differs between ERP, webshop and CRM.

What we build. A central PIM and one customer definition, synced to every system.

Result. All systems show the same data, and AI works from the truth.

Defines what a customer is

Challenge. Every system holds its own version of the customer, with duplicates and misshapen data.

What we build. One customer definition in our central system for core data, with records cleaned, merged and structured.

Result. Sales, service and finance see the same customer.

Keeps brand files in one library

Challenge. Logos, photos and brochures are scattered over drives and mailboxes.

What we build. A DAM where files are stored once, tagged, approved and linked to the product.

Result. Everyone uses the right, approved file.

Apps and dashboards for teams

Plans the week

Challenge. Planning lives in spreadsheets and shared calendars.

What we build. A planning board and agenda app built around your team.

Result. One clear view of who does what, and when.

Gives each role its dashboard

Challenge. Reports are scattered, and not everyone should see everything.

What we build. Dashboards per role, managed in administration.

Result. Managers see all, a sales employee does not see revenue.

Replaces costly software

Challenge. You pay for a large application and use a small part of it.

What we build. The features you need, rebuilt as an app on your own data.

Result. Lower cost, and a tool that fits your process.

Agents and apps instead of costly software

Writes content with a content agent

Challenge. Product texts, newsletters and posts are written by hand, and every channel says something different.

What we build. A content agent that writes from your aligned product and brand data, and a person approves.

Result. More content, consistent across channels, in less time.

Finds leads in your customer base

Challenge. Your customer base holds buyers who never hear from you, but nobody has time to look.

What we build. An agent that analyses customers and order history across your systems and lists the likely leads.

Result. Sales spends time on the customers most likely to buy.

Manages leave in an HR app

Challenge. Staff ask for leave by mail, and an expensive HR package does far more than you need.

What we build. A custom HR app where employees request and manage vacation, with approval for the manager.

Result. Leave is clear for everyone, without the licence cost.
