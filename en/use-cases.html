Orders and mail we automate

Reads orders from email and PDF

Challenge. Orders arrive by email and PDF and are typed in by hand.

What we build. An AI flow reads each order, matches customer and products, and creates it in your system.

Result. Less typing, fewer errors, and staff free for other work.

How does it work?
  1. An order arrives by email or PDF.
  2. The AI flow reads the text and attachment and pulls out customer, products and quantities.
  3. It looks up the customer and products in the one source, so prices are right.
  4. The order is created and a reply is ready.

Who stays in control. You or a colleague approve anything unclear. Only clear orders go through automatically.

Answers the inbox and complaints

Challenge. Mailboxes full of questions and complaints that need sorting and answering.

What we build. AI classifies each message, drafts a reply from your own data and passes difficult cases to a person.

Result. Quick, consistent replies, and nothing slips through.

How does it work?
  1. A message arrives in the inbox.
  2. The agent decides whether it is a question, complaint or order.
  3. It looks up the customer’s data and writes a reply.
  4. Routine questions go out directly, hard cases go to a colleague.

Who stays in control. You decide which messages may be answered automatically and which go past a person first.

Enters orders at the customer

Challenge. An account manager has to go back to the office to place an order.

What we build. An internal app where the account manager creates the order for the customer on the spot.

Result. The order is placed during the visit, with the right prices.

How does it work?
  1. The account manager opens the app at the customer.
  2. The customer and the right prices come from the one source.
  3. The order is filled in on the spot.
  4. On save, the order is in your system straight away.

Who stays in control. Prices and customer agreements are fixed, so the account manager cannot enter the wrong thing.

Data, customers and files

Keeps product and customer data equal

Challenge. Product and customer information differs between ERP, webshop and CRM.

What we build. A central PIM and one customer definition, synced to every system.

Result. All systems show the same data, and AI works from the truth.

How does it work?
  1. You change a product or price in one place.
  2. The platform passes the change on to the webshop, CRM and other systems.
  3. Every system then shows the same data.
  4. If anything differs, you see it straight away.

Who stays in control. One source decides what is right. All other systems follow.

Defines what a customer is

Challenge. Every system holds its own version of the customer, with duplicates and misshapen data.

What we build. One customer definition in our central system for core data, with records cleaned, merged and structured.

Result. Sales, service and finance see the same customer.

How does it work?
  1. We collect the customers from all your systems.
  2. Duplicate and deviating records are recognised.
  3. They are merged into one customer record.
  4. New customers follow the same definition from now on.

Who stays in control. You approve the first clean-up round. After that the platform keeps it up.

Keeps brand files in one library

Challenge. Logos, photos and brochures are scattered over drives and mailboxes.

What we build. A DAM where files are stored once, tagged, approved and linked to the product.

Result. Everyone uses the right, approved file.

How does it work?
  1. You upload files or link them from mail and drives.
  2. Each file gets tags and is linked to the right product or customer.
  3. A colleague approves files.
  4. Teams and flows only use approved files.

Who stays in control. Only approved versions are visible to your team and your agents.

Apps and dashboards for teams

Plans the week

Challenge. Planning lives in spreadsheets and shared calendars.

What we build. A planning board and agenda app built around your team.

Result. One clear view of who does what, and when.

How does it work?
  1. Planning comes from your orders, deliveries and customer appointments.
  2. You see the week on one planning board.
  3. A change to an order updates the planning.
  4. Everyone sees the same, current agenda.

Who stays in control. Your team adjusts the planning. The app follows how you work.

Gives each role its dashboard

Challenge. Reports are scattered, and not everyone should see everything.

What we build. Dashboards per role, managed in administration.

Result. Managers see all, a sales employee does not see revenue.

How does it work?
  1. The administrator chooses per role what is visible.
  2. Each employee logs in and sees only their own view.
  3. The numbers come from the same source for everyone.
  4. Changing a role is a matter of a switch.

Who stays in control. The administrator keeps control of access, for example to shield revenue and cost prices.

Replaces costly software

Challenge. You pay for a large application and use a small part of it.

What we build. The features you need, rebuilt as an app on your own data.

Result. Lower cost, and a tool that fits your process.

How does it work?
  1. We look at which part of your expensive software you actually use.
  2. We build those functions as an app on your own data.
  3. You test the app alongside your current package.
  4. Only when it works do you stop the licence.

Who stays in control. You decide when to switch. You are not obliged to switch anything off.

Agents and apps instead of costly software

Writes content with a content agent

Challenge. Product texts, newsletters and posts are written by hand, and every channel says something different.

What we build. A content agent that writes from your aligned product and brand data, and a person approves.

Result. More content, consistent across channels, in less time.

How does it work?
  1. The agent pulls product and brand data from the one source.
  2. It writes a text in your tone and style.
  3. The text goes to a colleague as a draft.
  4. After approval it goes to the channel where it belongs.

Who stays in control. Nothing is published without your approval.

Finds leads in your customer base

Challenge. Your customer base holds buyers who never hear from you, but nobody has time to look.

What we build. An agent that analyses customers and order history across your systems and lists the likely leads.

Result. Sales spends time on the customers most likely to buy.

How does it work?
  1. The agent reads customers and order history from the one source.
  2. It looks for patterns, such as customers who have not ordered for a while.
  3. It lines up the most promising leads.
  4. Sales gets a list with a suggested next step.

Who stays in control. Your team decides who to call or email. The agent only makes the list.

Manages leave in an HR app

Challenge. Staff ask for leave by mail, and an expensive HR package does far more than you need.

What we build. A custom HR app where employees request and manage vacation, with approval for the manager.

Result. Leave is clear for everyone, without the licence cost.

How does it work?
  1. An employee requests leave in the app.
  2. The manager gets a notification and approves or declines.
  3. On approval the calendar is updated.
  4. The employee’s balance is right straight away.

Who stays in control. You set the rules, such as number of days, yourself. No expensive HR package needed.
