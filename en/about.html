What is Moiz AI? In plain language.

Your IT partner used to set up your network. We set up your AI. Moiz AI makes your company ready to work with AI and keeps it running afterwards.

1. Your systems start talking to each other

Our platform connects your ERP, webshop, mail and customer data. The same information everywhere.

2. AI starts doing the work

Reading orders, answering mail, writing texts. Smart assistants take this work off your hands.

3. We manage it for you

You do not need any AI skills. We build it, keep it running and adjust it.
